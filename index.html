Daily limits

THC and nicotine limits are in puffs of whichever device is selected above, and convert when you switch. The alcohol limit is in cans. Counts reset at midnight. Leave blank or 0 for no limit.

Device equivalents

THC
1 waxpen puff =joint puffs
Alcohol
1 can in your count = 473 ml at 5% ≈ 1.39 standard drinks
Nicotine
1 vape puff =cigarette puffs

The THC and nicotine ratios are rough estimates, so adjust them to match your own gear. The alcohol conversion is fixed. It uses Canada’s standard drink, 17.05 ml of pure alcohol.

Alcohol container

Each drink counts as portion × (container ml ÷ 473) × (ABV ÷ 5). A full tallboy of 5% beer is 1 can. Half a tallboy at 8% is 0.8. A quarter of a 40% fifth is about 3.2 cans. A blank ABV uses 5%, a typical beer.
